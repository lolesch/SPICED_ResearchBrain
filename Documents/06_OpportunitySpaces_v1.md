## 1. Urgency & Decision Confidence

**Core HMW:** HMW 1  
**Problem space:** Users cannot assess risk → hesitation or anxiety → wrong or delayed action

**Opportunities:**

- Help users **classify issue severity**
- Provide **clear next steps (do / don’t)**
- Reduce fear of **overreacting vs underreacting**

**Evidence refs:**

- A1 00:00:44 (uncertainty about urgency)
- A3 00:00:24 (fear of danger)
- A3 00:02:30 (fear of wrong decision)

## 2. Guided Evidence Capture

**Core HMW:** HMW 3  
**Problem space:** Users don’t know what info matters → incomplete reports → back-and-forth

**Opportunities:**

- Define **“minimum viable evidence”**
- Guide **what to capture (photo, video, context)**
- Help users **structure observations**

**Evidence refs:**

- A1 00:01:28 (“don’t know what info they need”)
- A3 00:01:30 (“should I film it?”)
- A1 00:01:42 (desire for checklist)

---

## 3. Efficient Communication Output

**Core HMW:** HMW 2  
**Problem space:** Reporting is messy, repetitive, emotional → inefficient resolution

**Opportunities:**

- Generate **clear, structured messages**
- Reduce **back-and-forth loops**
- Help users **sound credible and calm**

**Evidence refs:**

- A1 00:01:28 (“eight messages”)
- A2 00:01:51 (“I start explaining… rewrite it”)
- A2 00:01:35 (desire for clarity)

---

## 4. Credibility & Issue History (“Receipts”)

**Core HMW:** HMW 4  
**Problem space:** Single reports lack weight → recurring issues get dismissed

**Opportunities:**

- Track **issues over time**
- Show **patterns & recurrence**
- Create **defensible documentation**

**Evidence refs:**

- A2 00:02:41 (“I want receipts”)
- A2 00:01:17 (photos are messy)
- A3 00:02:57 (need for history/patterns)

---

## 5. Reducing User Burden (Abstraction)

**Core HMW:** HMW 5  
**Problem space:** Users are forced to act like experts → stress + poor input quality

**Opportunities:**

- Reduce need for **manual interpretation**
- Translate inputs into **structured outputs**
- Shift from **user describes → system interprets**

**Evidence refs:**

- A3 00:01:19 (“hard to describe”)
- A1 00:01:07 (“didn’t know what to say”)
- A3 00:02:30 (responsibility without expertise)
