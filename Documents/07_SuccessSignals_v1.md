
### **1. Confident Action Taking**

**Observable / measurable indicators:**

- Reduced hesitation before acting
- Clear first action taken without external validation
- Users follow through on next steps independently

**We’ll know this works if…**

- Users no longer pause in uncertainty and instead take immediate, appropriate action
- Users explicitly state what they will do next (e.g., monitor, report, escalate) without second-guessing
- Users express reduced anxiety about “overreacting vs underreacting”

**Evidence refs:**

- “is this urgent or can it wait?” (00:00:44)
- “I don’t want to underreact and I don’t want to overreact” (00:02:30)
- “Just something that tells me what to do safely” (00:01:03)

---

### **2. Complete & Structured Reporting**

**Observable / measurable indicators:**

- Fewer back-and-forth messages
- Reports include all key details in one go
- Users rely on guidance/checklists when documenting issues

**We’ll know this works if…**

- Users send one clear, structured report instead of multiple follow-ups
- Users include relevant evidence (photos, context, timeline) without being prompted
- Users express confidence that they are providing “the right information”

**Evidence refs:**

- “it becomes like eight messages” (00:01:28)
- “I don’t know what information they actually need” (00:01:28)
- “here’s what to check, here’s what to capture, and here’s how to describe it” (00:01:42)
- “I need a checklist” (00:01:30)

---

### **3. Credible & Taken-Seriously Communication**

**Observable / measurable indicators:**

- Users feel confident their issue is understood
- Less emotional rewriting / over-explaining
- Reduced need to “prove” legitimacy

**We’ll know this works if…**

- Users send concise, calm messages without rewriting or over-explaining
- Users feel their reports are credible and not easily dismissed
- Users stop expressing the need to “prove” the issue over time

**Evidence refs:**

- “it feels like you have to prove you're not being irresponsible” (00:00:48)
- “I want something that helps me sound serious but calm” (00:01:51)
- “if I don’t have evidence, it's easy for someone to say it's fine” (00:01:19)
