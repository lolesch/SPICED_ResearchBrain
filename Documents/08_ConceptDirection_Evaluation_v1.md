# Concept Direction Evaluation (Against Personas + Success Signals)

## 1. Guided Diagnosis Flow

### Persona fit

**Cautious First-Time Reporter**

- ✅ Directly addresses hesitation and uncertainty
- ✅ Provides “what to do” clarity upfront
- ✅ Reduces back-and-forth via structured reporting

**Evidence Builder Under Pressure**

- ✅ Helps structure information correctly
- ⚠️ Less strong on long-term evidence building

---

### Success signal coverage

**1. Confident Action Taking**

- ✅ Strong
- Evidence: “is this urgent or can it wait?” (00:00:44)  
    → Directly solved via guided urgency classification

**2. Complete & Structured Reporting**

- ✅ Very strong
- Evidence: “I don’t know what information they actually need” (00:01:28)  
    → Checklist + guided capture directly solves this

**3. Credible & Taken-Seriously Communication**

- ✅ Strong
- Evidence: “I want something that helps me sound serious but calm” (00:01:51)  
    → Structured output improves tone + clarity

---

### Verdict

👉 **Best overall coverage across ALL success signals**  
👉 Strongest alignment with **core HMW (urgency → action)**
