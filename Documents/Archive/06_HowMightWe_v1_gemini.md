# List of HMW Questions

## Broad
### HMW 1
- How might we empower tenants to feel like experts in their own living space?
    - **Diagnose:** Too broad. While it captures the emotional goal of Persona 1, it doesn't point toward the "evidence" or "communication" problems specifically.

### HMW 2
- How might we transform the tenant-landlord relationship from an adversarial one into a collaborative one?
    - **Diagnose:** Disconnected from PS#4. This focuses on the social relationship rather than the specific task of capturing and providing evidence to solve the credibility gap.

## Specific
### HMW 3
- How might we help Persona 1 (Cautious Reporter) identify the "minimum viable evidence" needed to stop back-and-forth messaging?
    - **Diagnose:** Good scope. It directly addresses the "eight messages" frustration and the lack of expertise mentioned in the problem statement.

### HMW 4
- How might we assist Persona 2 (Evidence Builder) in documenting the progression of recurring issues over time?
    - **Diagnose:** Good scope. Directly addresses the need for "receipts" and "history" noted in the evidence for Persona 2.

## Assumption-Challenging
### HMW 5
- How might we make the evidence so objective that the tenant doesn't need to "describe" the issue at all?
    - **Diagnose:** Challenging. We assume the user needs to _improve_ their descriptions; this explores if we can automate the description entirely (e.g., via visual analysis) to remove the need for tenant expertise.

### Quality Check & Validation

- **Persona Alignment:** These questions directly address the **Inefficiency** (Persona 1) and **Leverage/Credibility** (Persona 2) issues from [[05_ProtoPersonas_v1]] .

- **Problem Statement Fit:** Every question circles back to the core tension of "Expertise vs. Action" found in [[04_ProblemStatements_v3#Final Statement Decision|PS#4]].

- **Strict Formula Check:**
    - "How might we" + **Action + **User** + **Outcome**?
    - _Example:_ "How might we *assist* [[05_ProtoPersonas_v1#Proto-Persona 2 Evidence Builder Under Pressure|Persona 2]] *in documenting progression over time*?" → **Pass.**
